Good to know

Before you visit.

Do I need an appointment?

Booking ahead helps us give you unrushed time. You can also call to check same-day availability.

Can I bring an existing prescription?

Yes. Bring your current prescription or previous glasses if you have them. Our optician will review them with you.

Do you see children and older adults?

Yes. We welcome all ages and can help you choose an appointment suited to your family.

Can I order contact lenses here?

Ask our optician during your visit about available lens options and suitability.
