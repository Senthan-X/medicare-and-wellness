GOOD TO KNOW

Questions before you begin.

Does everyone need braces or aligners?

No. An orthodontic consultation helps determine whether treatment is appropriate for your needs. Your clinician will discuss options and alternatives.

How long does treatment take?

Timing varies by person and treatment plan. Your clinician can provide an estimate after an assessment and explain what may affect it.

Can I decide after the first visit?

Yes. The first visit is for questions, assessment and understanding your options. You can take time to consider the plan.

What should I bring?

Bring any relevant dental records or questions you have. If you are booking for a child, a parent or guardian should attend.
