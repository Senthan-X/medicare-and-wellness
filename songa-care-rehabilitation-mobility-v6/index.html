A FEW HELPFUL DETAILS

Good to know before you begin.

Do I need a referral?

Requirements vary by service and local rules. Contact your clinician or local health provider to confirm what applies to you.

How long does a visit take?

Your first appointment length depends on your goals and the service. We will explain timing when you arrange a visit.

Can someone come with me?

Yes. You are welcome to bring a support person if that helps you feel comfortable.
