GOOD QUESTIONS, HONEST ANSWERS

A few things
you might wonder.

Can I stay with my child?

Of course. You’re welcome beside them throughout the visit.

What if my child feels nervous?

We go slowly, explain what is happening and pause whenever your child needs. A first visit can simply be a hello.

What should we bring?

Bring any questions, your child’s health details and a favorite comfort item if that helps.
